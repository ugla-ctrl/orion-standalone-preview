Business model

Orion earns when events run on it

In 2027 IMXP is the first customer. That sets the price before anyone else pays it.

2027: the internal model

IMXP pays Orion a license for each event, plus a small fee on the money that moves through it.

4IMXP events
$30Mrevenue and payouts through Orion
~$1MOrion revenue

After 2027: how we expect to monetize

  • Other producers. The same license and fee, for festivals that want what IMXP has.
  • Small events. A self-serve version for retreats, workshops and small festivals.
  • Priced from proof. 2027 results set the terms, not guesses.

Event revenue and risk stay with the producer. 2027 figures from IMXP's event cash flows, October 2026; the intercompany terms are being finalized at arm's length.
