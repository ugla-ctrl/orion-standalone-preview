Where it started

In August 2026, IMXP brought 4,200 people from more than 75 countries to the edge of Iceland to stand under a total solar eclipse.

4,200Participants from 75+ countries
$6MEvent revenue supported
120+Decisions on record
2 of 42027 events vetted by Orion

Behind a gathering like that sit thousands of decisions: vendors, lead times, budgets, permits, crews, approvals. Most of them still live in spreadsheets, group chats and people's heads. Plans start from zero every time, demand is a guess until tickets sell, and when the event ends, what the crew learned leaves with them.

That isn't only our problem. Across live entertainment, almost every dollar goes back out the door as cost. In 2025, 97 cents of every Live Nation concert dollar went to costs, even at a record 159 million fans.

So we built the thing we wished we'd had. Orion ran Iceland Eclipse from planning through show days, and it is now planning our next four events.

How it works

Every event, sized to what's really possible.

Orion turns an idea on paper into a plan the team can actually deliver. It checks the money, the people and the risk before the big spend, then keeps everything moving in the background.

  1. Test demand first

    Pre-sales and waitlists prove the event before anyone commits to the expensive parts.

  2. Plan from real numbers

    Real lead times, real vendor costs and each team's work for today, drawn from the events before it.

  3. Keep what crews learn

    Every decision is saved with who made it, why, and what happened next, so the next event starts ahead.

Watch Orion run a real festival

Every event teaches the next

Our own events are the lab.

Each IMXP event in 2027 ships part of Orion and tests it with real crews, real money and a date that can't move. Then the same system opens to other producers.

  1. Mar 20 to 22 · AustinCoherence

    Orion beta: live demand testing and daily planning with the festival team.

  2. May 7 to 9 · RoswellATOMIKA

    The first full release, with vendor and crew approvals and payouts running through Orion.

  3. Jul 9 to 11 · ReykjavíkMidnight Sun

    The memory test: an event that starts from what Orion learned at the last ones.

  4. Jul 30 to Aug 9 · LuxorEgypt Eclipse

    Our largest event, run end to end on Orion, from demand to settlement.

After Egypt, Orion goes to its first outside producer, then to the long tail of small gatherings: retreats, workshops and community festivals that have never had tools like this.
